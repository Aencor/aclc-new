Dev note · Conversion model changedThis replaces the discovery-call booking as the primary ACLC conversion. It lowers friction — an email costs the visitor far less than committing to 45 minutes — but it moves ACLC from a hard bottom-of-funnel conversion to a softer capture, so the site metric changes from calls booked to registrations, and a nurture sequence now has to carry people from registration to enrolment. The call has not disappeared: it is an opt-in tickbox for high-intent visitors, which is also the cleanest lead-scoring signal on the site. Fire generate_lead with interest and language as parameters, and a separate call_requested event when the box is ticked.

Privacy policy

Last updated: to be confirmed on publication.

This page is a working outline. The final wording must be reviewed by counsel before launch — the sections below set out what has to be covered, not the final text.

Who we are

Advanced Coaching & Leadership Center, Inc., 1400 Camp Letoli Road, Saint Jo, Texas 76265, United States. Contact: the address on our contact page.

What we collect

Information you give us directly when you register for programme information, subscribe to the Thought of the Week, contact us or enrol in a programme: name, email address, telephone number where you provide it, preferred language, and the programme interests you select. Information collected automatically: device and browser data, pages visited, and referral source, through the analytics and advertising cookies described in the cookie policy.

Why we use it

To send you the information you asked for; to deliver programmes you enrol in; to answer your enquiries; to measure how our sites are used so we can improve them; and, where you have consented, to send you the weekly newsletter. We do not sell personal data.

Assessment data

Where you complete a Zones assessment, your answers and resulting profile are personal data. They are used to produce your result, to inform coaching if you enrol, and are not published or shared without your written consent.

Who we share it with

Service providers who process data on our behalf: our website host, email platform, payment processor, analytics provider and course platform. Each is bound to process data only on our instructions. A full list of processors must be maintained and published here.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and subscription records for as long as you remain subscribed plus a defined retention period; enrolment and financial records for the period required by law. Exact retention periods to be set with counsel.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of the European Union are covered by the GDPR, residents of California by the CCPA, and residents of Mexico by the LFPDPPP. Requests are handled at the contact address above.

International transfers

We operate from the United States. If you contact us from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States under appropriate safeguards.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a revised date.
